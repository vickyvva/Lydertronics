WHO WE ARE

Company overview

We combine human review, structured workflows and technology to help teams prepare, assess and improve data used in AI systems.

Lydertronics AI is an India-based AI data and technology services company headquartered in Hyderabad, Telangana. We work across data operations and applied AI, supporting projects that require reliable human input, clear guidelines and measurable quality checks.

Our work can be scoped for model training, evaluation, computer vision, speech and language systems, robotics research and enterprise workflows. Each engagement is planned around the client's data, acceptance criteria, privacy requirements and delivery schedule.
